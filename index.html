Financial Literacy: Teaching Beyond Conventional Learning

Financial education in schools is minimal. We help at-risk youth and low-income families avoid debt and financial anxiety through practical money skills.

  • •Workshops on budgeting, saving, and avoiding predatory lending.
  • •Targeted mentorship for young adults under financial stress.
  • •Teaching the ethics of wealth, stewardship, and generosity.
