Faith-Driven Compassion

Serving Hong Kong with Love and Purpose.

We are a group of Hong Kongers who have been deeply blessed by this city. Having lived and worked here for years, we believe it is time to give back. Rooted in the teachings of Christ, we seek to deliver a message of love through practical service—empowering the vulnerable, serving our seniors, and building a more cohesive community.

"Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me."

Our Story & Founders

CK Charity Foundation was born out of gratitude. Our founders have spent decades living and working in Hong Kong, benefiting enormously from the stability, opportunities, and community this city offers. We believe that success is not just about what we accumulate, but what we give back.

Our Mission

Our mission is to embody the love of Christ by addressing both the practical and spiritual needs of Hong Kong’s marginalized and aging populations.

Core Focus Areas

What We Do

We focus on three core areas where our professional backgrounds and faith-driven compassion can make the biggest difference.

Financial Literacy: Teaching Beyond Conventional Learning

Financial education in Hong Kong schools is minimal. Many people struggle with debt and financial anxiety because they were never taught basic money management.

  • •Workshops on budgeting, saving, and avoiding predatory lending.
